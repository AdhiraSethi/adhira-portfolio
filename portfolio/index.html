About

I love coding. There's nothing quite like starting with a blank file and ending up with something that works, and that's what pulled me into computer science. These days I'm most excited by the place where software meets AI and data: taking a messy real-world problem, working out what the data is telling me, and turning it into a product people can actually use. VoxIntel

An agentic email-triage system. A 12-step pipeline reads each email for intent, sentiment, emotion and priority, then a RAG module drafts a context-aware reply with a confidence score. Low-confidence or high-priority emails go to a manager for review. Served as a REST API with FastAPI.
